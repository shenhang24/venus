# Support

For setup and usage, start with the [README](README.md), [deployment guide](docs/DEPLOYMENT.md), and [page tour](docs/PAGE-TOUR.md). For bugs or feature requests, search [existing issues](https://github.com/shenhang24/venus/issues) and use an [issue template](https://github.com/shenhang24/venus/issues/new/choose).

Venus is maintained by an individual. Support is best-effort; response times, fixes, and feature delivery are not guaranteed. There is no dedicated help desk or advertised private support channel.

Include your Venus version, operating system, Node.js version, minimal steps, and expected versus actual behavior. Use synthetic Markdown to demonstrate a problem. Redact screenshots and logs before sharing; never upload a private Vault, conversation history, knowledge or skill collection, concept records, credentials, tokens, or account details. Do not purchase API credits or send private material just to obtain support.

Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for security concerns and [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) for participation rules. Account billing and third-party service outages should be handled through the relevant provider's own support resources; the Venus maintainer cannot access those accounts.
