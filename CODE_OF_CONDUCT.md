# Code of conduct

This policy applies to Venus issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces administered by the maintainer.

Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and code, explain disagreements, welcome questions, and respect different experiences and backgrounds. Do not engage in harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, doxxing, or repeated disruption. Do not publish anyone's private material or credentials.

## Reporting and enforcement

For conduct concerns that can be described safely in public, open an [issue](https://github.com/shenhang24/venus/issues/new) with minimal necessary context. Do not repeat abusive content, identify a private individual, or expose sensitive details. There is currently no designated private conduct-reporting channel; do not post confidential reports publicly.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock discussions, restrict participation, or block accounts where repository controls allow. Responses depend on the context and severity; the maintainer may act without a prior warning. Maintenance and moderation are best-effort, with no guaranteed response time. This policy does not create a confidential investigation service.
